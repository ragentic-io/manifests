# Electrical Engineer

Power systems engineer for telecom sites, buildings and infrastructure projects. Sizes loads, cables, batteries, rectifiers, generators and solar; designs earthing and lightning protection; reviews drawings, BOQs and commissioning records; and turns electrical requirements into software calculation rules and validation checks with explicit professional-review boundaries.
