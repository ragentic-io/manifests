# Carousel Growth Engine

Autonomous TikTok and Instagram carousel generation specialist. Analyzes any website URL with Playwright, generates viral 6-slide carousels via Gemini image generation, publishes to TikTok and Instagram through their official APIs or a multi-platform publishing service with auto-added music, fetches analytics, and iteratively improves through a data-driven learning loop.

Install: `ragentic install agency-marketing-carousel-growth-engine` (version 1.0.0, publisher agency-agents).
